How it behaves

AUTO — pump follows its timer periods. MAN — runs continuously, START/STOP buttons take over. OFF — dead, STOP lamp lit.
The white STOP lamp logic: RUN lamp lit while running, red STOP lamp lit while stopped. Heat pump ON lamp only lights when its selector is ON and at least one circulation pump is running (no flow, no heating).
Tap a TRIP lamp to simulate an overload trip — the pump drops out until you tap it again to reset.

The dial beside each timer is what the Hager EH011 in the plant room should look like right now, with these ON periods set. Hold your phone next to the real unit and compare: the hour against the ▶ pointer should match, and the blue tappets should be pushed in over the same hours. If the real dial reads a different time, the pump is running to the wrong clock — correct it and note it down.
